This branch turns the first-playable prototype into a connected, six-plot Brainrot farm with stacked producer displays, a complete collect/process/merge loop, and a responsive HUD. Chicken Farm references guide the map proportions and visual hierarchy; rewards, dimensions and capacity are explicit adaptations.

## Behavior

- **Map and characters:** five rows support 50 producers per plot. Thick island foundations, square silos, mirrored farmer stalls, waist-height counters, fences and grounded bridges connect to the hub. Sloped shelf dividers, a 24×16 collection tray, clearer farm titles, producer-name colors, two-sided current-server leaderboards and count-first processor boards improve the player view. Native lawn surfaces replace redundant ornament Parts while retaining the collision foundation. The original 100-frame study and separate Foltyn study retain annotated comparisons.
- **Farm loop:** up to 96 ordinary Minis per plot share one airborne/ground limit. Nearby Minis enter the bag during flight or after landing; full-pad entry supports buying, depositing, claiming cash, upgrading and merging, with bounded held repeats. Progressive prices, six species, Lucky rewards and rebirth remain server-owned. The ten-checkpoint obby validates movement and awards an additive +0.25x Mini-production bonus for five minutes, with a ten-minute reward cooldown.
- **HUD, settings and audio:** Home and onboarding remain accessible; Lucky and Settings preserve movement and collection. Touch layouts leave space for Roblox's actual Jump/joystick controls, including control-size changes and scrolling short cards. Session preferences independently control music and effects. One quiet music voice and three pooled action voices use verified loading, bounded playback and failure handling.
- **Resource work:** spatial Mini-neighbor queries and conservative offscreen animation scheduling reduce repeated work. Transaction snapshots remain deep; outgoing packets avoid duplicate nested copies. Hidden menu construction, unrelated Shop redraws, duplicate Mini endpoint writes and repeated pad-detail scans are removed. Scoped type annotations retain strict checking without claiming a warning-free project.
