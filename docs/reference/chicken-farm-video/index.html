Applied to this update

The notes below connect each captured moment to a concrete Brainrot implementation. Green notes identify what is now in the local Studio build; the original observations remain available for comparison.

1 · Typography and HUD proportions

Shared Fredoka One styling, dark outlines, cream action names, lime prices and colored species names. Larger woven Mini basket, compact Lucky odds card and two-tile rebirth summary. This is our chosen matching style; the video's exact font asset is unverified.

2 · Plot composition and readable boards

The rear shelf now spans about 90% of the conveyor width. Pads separate action names from prices; the processor board exposes only implemented status and rate. The blank group board now has its own readable front face. Mirrored utilities, fences and the red collection tray remain intact.

3 · Deposit and pickup feedback

A broader warm deposit halo preserves visible studs. Gold pickup amounts aggregate into at most six popups. Cash bursts use brighter curved trails and smaller repeats; deposit portraits replace previous bursts instead of accumulating.

4 · Progression and sound pass

Rebirth shows actual Buy50 and cash-multiplier rewards. Higher tiers have sparse local accents with a 16-producer limit. Success cues reuse the project's existing cleared sound with quiet pitch patterns and three pooled voices. No audio was extracted from the reference video.
